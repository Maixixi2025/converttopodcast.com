Why ConvertToPodcast

7 reasons it stands out as a free AI podcast generator in 2026 — and the honest comparison against NotebookLM and ElevenLabs.

Most AI podcast tools ask you to upload your own files and accept whatever the model gives back. ConvertToPodcast does that — and a few things that the bigger names either lock behind a paywall or simply don't do yet. Here are the seven reasons it earns a permanent spot in your reading-to-listening workflow.

1. Free 30 credits per month, no credit card

The free tier gives you 30 credits every month, with 1 credit equaling 1 minute of generated audio. That's roughly 15 short podcasts or 3–4 full-length ones, every month, for free — and you don't need to enter a credit card to sign up. The free plan resets on the same day each month, and there's no expiration on the credits you do earn. Most alternatives either require a credit card for the free trial or cap you at 3 generations total. ConvertToPodcast's free tier is built for habitual use, not just a one-time test drive.

2. Two-host conversation style, not robotic TTS

ConvertToPodcast doesn't just read your article aloud. It generates a real two-host conversation — one host explains, the other asks questions, and they take turns like a real show. The output sounds closer to a podcast you would actually want to listen to than to a screen reader or a TTS voice cloning your text. Three styles are available on the free tier: Deep Dive, Quick Summary, and Debate. Each style changes the script structure, not just the voices. Paid plans unlock additional styles including Interview and Lecture.

3. Three input formats, one tab

Paste a URL, drop a PDF, or type text directly — all from the same input panel on the homepage. The tabs let you switch between formats without losing your other settings (style, language, length). This matters when you read the same article from three different sources in a week: the URL on Monday, the PDF report on Wednesday, and a quick notes file on Friday. One tool, three entry points, no need to maintain bookmarks for three different converters.

4. Five podcast styles

The script generation is style-aware, not just voice-aware. Deep Dive produces a 10–15 minute explainer with two experts working through the material methodically. Quick Summary compresses the same content into a 1-minute rapid recap. Debate generates two perspectives arguing the topic, which is useful for essays, op-eds, and any content where you want to hear the other side. Interview and Lecture (paid plans) work better for transcripts, course material, and research papers.

5. Five languages, no extra cost

English, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, and Portuguese are all available on the free tier. The language setting changes both the script language and the voice — so a Spanish article produces a Spanish podcast, not a Spanish-scripted English podcast. The voices are picked for natural prosody in each language; they are not the same voice model with a different accent. Two more languages (German, French) are in active development.

6. MP3 download on all paid plans, commercial rights included

Every paid plan includes an MP3 download button. The audio is yours: you can publish it on a podcast feed, embed it in a blog post, or use it in client work without paying royalties. Free tier audio is for personal use only — a fair trade for the 30 free credits per month. Pro and Ultra plans upgrade the voice quality to ElevenLabs-grade natural speech, which is the level you would expect from a top-tier audio production.

7. No tracking, no ads, content deleted on request

ConvertToPodcast is funded by paid plans, not by selling your reading history. There are no third-party tracking scripts, no retargeting pixels, and no ad networks. Your submitted URLs, PDFs, and text are processed to generate the podcast and stored in your personal history so you can re-listen or download later. You can delete any individual generation from your account history, and you can request full account deletion by emailing support. The full privacy policy is on the privacy page.

How ConvertToPodcast compares to NotebookLM and ElevenLabs

The honest answer: each tool has a different center of gravity, and the best one depends on what you are trying to do.

vs. Google NotebookLM

NotebookLM is the gold standard for "I have a pile of my own sources, give me an audio overview." It is excellent at synthesizing from your uploaded documents and produces a polished, contextual summary. The limits: NotebookLM only accepts sources you upload (no arbitrary URL, no plain text), the Audio Overview feature is only available in English, and there is no way to download the audio as an MP3. ConvertToPodcast covers what NotebookLM doesn't — URL input, plain text input, 5 languages, and MP3 export on paid plans.

vs. ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is the gold standard for voice quality and voice cloning. If you need a specific voice, or you are producing audio at a level where the voice quality is the differentiator (audiobooks, branded content, high-end podcasts), ElevenLabs is the right tool. ConvertToPodcast uses ElevenLabs voices on the Pro and Ultra plans, so you are not choosing between the two — you are choosing how much voice customization you need. The free tier uses Cartesia and other providers, which are noticeably less natural but adequate for casual listening.

Yes. The free tier gives 30 credits per month (1 credit = 1 minute of generated audio) and does not require a credit card. Paid plans start at $4.99 per month for 300 credits and MP3 download.

Most podcasts are generated in under 2 minutes for a 10-minute output. Very long PDFs (100+ pages) can take up to 5 minutes.
